Encoder: Reads and understands the input.
Does not predict the next word.
Used for text understanding tasks.

Decoder: Generates new text using context.
Predicts the next word one by one.
Used for text generation tasks.

Pre-training = learns general learning
Fine-tuning = learns task-specific learning.
Pre-training gives a model general understanding of language.
fine-tuning specializes it for a specific task.
