Normalization :-
Normalization in NLP means cleaning and standardizing text so that different forms of the same word or text are treated consistently by a model.
Tokens:
Tokens are the smallest units of text that an NLP model works with.
Tokenization = breaking text into tokens.
Tokenization means breaking text into smaller units called tokens.
